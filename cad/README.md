# DSH-Prose side stylus clips

The two open TPU clips mount to the left edge when facing the screen. The stylus presses in sideways and is exposed between the clips so you can pull it out by the barrel. The raised rear tube is removed. The curved navy back, recessed logo, three front buttons and Rev D PCB fit are carried over. The shell is 120.4 × 202 × 18.8 mm. Installed clips add about 12.5 mm to its width.

Print one frame, one back, three front keycaps and two copies of `04-TPU-Side-Clip.stl`. Dry-fit the clip mounting tabs in the side pockets and secure them with an adhesive rated for the clip and shell materials. Let it cure before inserting the pen. The shell pockets are shallow and blind. Use the Rev D assembly instructions for the panel, PCB and fasteners.

The reference pen is Ø9.7 × 155.5 mm. Open `DSH-Prose-Side-Stylus.step` in Fusion and import `Stylus-Fit-Reference.step` at the same origin to see it parked. Clearance checks cover the enclosure, board, modeled hardware and parked pen. The TPU jaws flex during insertion. Verify that the actual pen snaps in and stays in place before relying on the clips. The clips provide mechanical storage only.
